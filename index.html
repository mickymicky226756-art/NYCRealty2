Frequently Asked
How do I deposit funds?
Go to Profile → Deposit, choose an amount, transfer to one of the listed bank accounts, then submit your transaction reference. Funds are usually credited within minutes.
When can I withdraw my earnings?
Daily returns can be collected from My Products. Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours to your registered bank account.
What is the minimum deposit?
The minimum deposit is 600 ETB. You can invest in any property package that fits your budget.
What is the minimum withdrawal?
The minimum withdrawal is 50 ETB. Withdrawal requests below this amount will not be processed.
How does the referral program work?
Share your invite link from the Team page. You earn 37% of your Level 1 invites' first investment, plus 3% (L2) and 1% (L3) on subsequent investments.
Is my account secure?
Yes. We use Firebase authentication and encrypted data storage to keep your account safe. Withdrawals are processed exclusively to your registered Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE) account.
